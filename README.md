### 📌 Project Objective
*  The goal was to determine how to allocate 1 cr investment to maximize operational   efficiency, customer satisfaction, and profitability. The analysis will consider historical   call data, performance metrics, and market trends to make informed decisions.

### 🔍 Key Insights
* Key Gap Identified: High volume of repeat calls (4879 total, from 275 repeat callers), indicating  efficiency issues.
* Performance : High number of sessions were rated 3 or below representing the disappointmentamong users .Even some gurus got rated well too proving their skills .
* Opportunity: Targeted investment in technology, training, and incentives will improve overall
